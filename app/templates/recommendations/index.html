Comprehensive Residential Energy Guidelines
High Tier
Load Shedding & Heavy Appliances
  • Set air conditioning thermostats to 24°C-26°C.
  • Turn off electric water heaters once warm.
  • Avoid running heavy induction cookers or ovens simultaneously during evening peak hours (6 PM – 10 PM).
  • Inspect refrigeration gaskets for cold air leaks.
Moderate Tier
Appliance & Standby Optimization
  • Unplug entertainment systems, chargers, and microwaves to stop phantom draw.
  • Run washing machines with full loads and cold water settings.
  • Switch all incandescent or halogen bulbs to LED luminaires.
Low Tier
Green Habit Sustainability
  • Maximize natural cross-ventilation and daylighting during daylight hours.
  • Keep tracking daily consumption in this portal to detect any unexpected surges early.
  • Sustain optimal energy habits across all household members.
